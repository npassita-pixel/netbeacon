How to use

1

Show bookmarks bar

Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Shift+B (Mac) to toggle the bookmarks bar in Chrome.

2

Drag the button

Click and drag the blue Beacon Scanner button above into your bookmarks bar. You'll see a small icon appear.

3

Visit any page

Navigate to any website you want to audit — your own site, a competitor, or any public URL.

4

Click the bookmark

Click Beacon Scanner in your bookmarks bar. A side panel will slide in with your accessibility score, issues, and fixes.
